I'm a developer and greyhound punter and I've built an open-source AI tool called
Greyhound-Agent. Every morning it reads the official Australian form guide PDFs, pulls out
around 75 features per dog (times, box draws, track history, trainer stats, margins, etc.),
then runs them through a machine-learning ensemble of three models — Random Forest, Gradient
Boosting, and XGBoost — to produce win probabilities for every dog in every race.

The whole thing takes about 20 minutes to train on a standard laptop and the code is free on
GitHub: https://github.com/danieljohnconstantine-a11y/Greyhound-Agent

It spits out two files each morning:
  • best_bets_report.txt — every race ranked by how confident the model is, with the top pick
    of the day highlighted up front.
  • track_ensemble_predictions.xlsx — a full Excel sheet with win percentages for every dog
    across every track running that day.

WHAT IS GREYHOUND-AGENT?
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Greyhound-Agent is an open-source Python pipeline that:

  1. Reads the official Australian greyhound form guides (PDF) from Greyhound Racing Victoria,
     Greyhound Racing NSW, Greyhound Racing SA, RWWA (WA), and Tasracing each morning.

  2. Extracts ~75 features per dog from those form guides — including recent race times,
     box draws, track history, trainer performance, margins, and more.

  3. Runs those features through a three-model ML ensemble trained on historical race results:
       • Random Forest (RF)
       • Gradient Boosting (GB)
       • XGBoost (XGB)
     All three models use sigmoid probability calibration so the win-probability outputs are
     accurate and consistent across tracks.

  4. Averages the three probability estimates and ranks every dog in every race.

  5. Produces two daily output files:
       • best_bets_report.txt  — every race ranked by "score gap" between the #1 and #2 dog.
         The larger the gap, the more confident the model is. The single best bet of the day
         is highlighted at the top.
       • track_ensemble_predictions.xlsx  — the full probability breakdown for every dog in
         every race, across all tracks, in a clean Excel spreadsheet.

THE SERVICE IDEA — DAILY EMAIL FOR LESS THAN $1 A MONTH
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

I'm exploring whether there is enough interest to offer a simple daily email subscription:

  • Each morning (before races begin) subscribers receive:
      – best_bets_report.txt   (plain text, easy to read on any device)
      – track_ensemble_predictions.xlsx  (Excel — all tracks, all races, all probabilities)

  • Pricing: AU$0.03 per email (i.e. per race day).
    In any calendar month with 31 race days the total cost is $0.93 — always under $1.00.
    That works out to roughly $0.60–$0.90/month depending on how many days racing runs.

To my knowledge, there is currently no service that delivers Australian greyhound form data
in Excel format on a daily basis at this price point. Most form services charge $10–$50/month
or lock predictions behind expensive subscription tiers.

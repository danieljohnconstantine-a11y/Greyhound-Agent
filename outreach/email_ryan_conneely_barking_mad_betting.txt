Hi Ryan,

Just wanted to say I genuinely enjoy watching your channel — the energy you bring to greyhound
content is great and I always get a kick out of it.

I'm not a developer by any stretch — just a greyhound fan who got a bit obsessed with the idea
of using AI to predict race outcomes. I've been building something called Greyhound-Agent using
AI tools to help me along the way, and I thought it might be worth sharing.

Every morning it reads the official Australian form guide PDFs and uses machine learning to come
up with win probabilities for every dog in every race. To be honest, the prediction side of
things has had mixed results — I'm definitely not claiming this is a money-making system! But
what I think is actually pretty useful is the Excel output. It converts the form data into a
spreadsheet with percentages for every dog across every track each day, which makes it really
easy for people to apply their own filters and build their own systems from scratch.

The code is free on GitHub if anyone wants to have a look:
https://github.com/danieljohnconstantine-a11y/Greyhound-Agent

I'm testing a simple daily email subscription that sends out the Excel file each morning before
the races — all for under $1 a month ($0.03 per race day). The idea is more about giving punters
a head start with data in a usable format rather than telling them who to back.

So I'm not a developer at all — I'm just a regular guy who loves the odds on greyhounds and

probabilities for every dog at every track. The idea is that people can take that data and build
their own filters and systems in a format they're actually comfortable with — Excel is something
most people already know how to use.

It's all free on GitHub if anyone wants to have a poke around:

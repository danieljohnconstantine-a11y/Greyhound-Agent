Great checklist — short answer: we have strong coverage on early pace/box/race-shape and trainer-rest cycles, but only partial coverage on weather/true track variant, class-transition deltas, and straight↔circle transition behavior.

Current coverage status:

1) Split Times & Early Sectional Speed vs Overall Time
✅ Mostly covered now
- SectionalSec, EarlySpeedIndex, EarlySpeedPercentile, TimeVsField, SpeedVsField, PaceBoxFactor, and InterferenceRisk are active.
- This means we are not relying only on final time.

2) Track Bias & Surface Condition Changes (weather/moisture/maintenance)
⚠️ Partial
- Track bias and upset factors are in (TrackBox* features, TrackUpsetFactor, surface heuristic).
- But live weather/meeting variant is not fully integrated in the core training flow (TrackConditionAdj remains neutral in core path).

3) Box Draw Map Interferences / Run-Style Clashes
✅ Mostly covered
- Implemented via pace-map logic, neighbor pressure, contested-lead modeling, InterferenceRisk, CompetitorDensity, and CompetitorAdjustment.

4) Trainer/Kennel Form Cycles + Days Since Last Start
✅ Covered well
- TrainerStrikeRate, TrainerMomentum, DLR/FreshnessFactor, DLW/WinStreak factors are all in current features.

5) Grade Transitions & Class Drops
⚠️ Partial
- GradeFactor, RTCFactor, and ClassRating exist.
- Explicit class-drop/rise delta (current grade vs avg last 3 grade levels) is not yet a dedicated feature.

6) Straight ↔ Circular Track Transition
⚠️ Partial
- Track-specific models and distinct straight/circle track handling exist (e.g., Richmond vs Richmond Straight; Murray Bridge vs Murray Bridge Straight).
- But explicit dog-level transition feature (recent straight runs then bend, or vice versa) is not yet engineered.

Bottom line:
- Strongly implemented: 1, 3, 4
- Partially implemented / next upgrades: 2, 5, 6

If you want, next patch can add the missing three explicitly:
1) live weather/track-variant adjustment in core training,
2) class rise/drop delta feature,
3) explicit straight↔circle transition feature per dog.

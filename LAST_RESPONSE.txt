How to improve prediction success with your current data (practical plan)

Short answer:
Yes — there ARE ways to improve hit rate with the data you now have.
The key is to stop optimizing only for "overall accuracy" and optimize for "bet quality + strike rate" by confidence band and track.

What will likely improve winning most (highest impact first):

1) Track-specific thresholding (instead of one global threshold)
- Keep current models, but set different bet thresholds per track.
- Example: only bet when model confidence >= X and gap >= Y for that specific track.
- Why: each track has different race shape, field quality, and variance.
- Expected benefit: fewer low-quality bets, better strike rate.

2) Confidence-band policy from your own history
- Use your best_bets_report history to define "bet bands":
  - Band A (high edge): strongest gap/confidence bands
  - Band B (medium edge)
  - Band C (avoid)
- You already have evidence that some gap buckets outperform others.
- Expected benefit: better selection discipline and less noise betting.

3) Time-based retraining and backtesting (walk-forward)
- Train on older dates, test on newer dates only (no leakage).
- Re-run monthly/fortnightly retrain windows.
- Compare old vs new model on unseen dates before promoting.
- Expected benefit: avoids overfitting and keeps model aligned with current form cycles.

4) Per-track model family weighting (RF/GB/XGB)
- Do not always weight RF/GB/XGB equally on every track.
- Some tracks may perform better with GB-heavy or XGB-heavy blend.
- Learn weights from validation per track and update config.
- Expected benefit: stronger top-pick quality where one model type dominates.

5) Objective function aligned to betting outcomes
- Evaluate by:
  - Top-pick win rate
  - Top-3 strike rate
  - ROI proxy (if odds available)
  - Calibration quality by confidence bucket
- Do not rely only on classifier accuracy.
- Expected benefit: tuning decisions match real betting goals.

6) Data quality hardening (very important)
- Strictly filter malformed outcomes (e.g., non-numeric anomalies like "Mon").
- Keep consistent track naming and race numbering.
- Continue excluding races without matching form PDFs.
- Expected benefit: less label noise -> cleaner learning signal.

7) Controlled bet volume (risk control)
- Even with better models, edge is uneven by day.
- Cap daily bets to top-N highest edge races only.
- Expected benefit: improves realized strike rate and bankroll stability.

What this means for your current setup:
- The new best_bets_reports workflow is useful.
- But the biggest jump will come from adding:
  1) per-track thresholds,
  2) per-band betting rules,
  3) walk-forward validation gate before each model release.

Realistic expectation:
- You can improve practical winning probability and consistency.
- But no model can guarantee constant improvement every run.
- Aim for stable edge over many races, not single-day outcomes.
